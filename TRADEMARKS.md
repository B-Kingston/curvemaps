# CurveMaps name and logo

The CurveMaps source code is source-available under the PolyForm Shield
License 1.0.0 (see `LICENSE`). That licence covers the code. It gives you no
right to use the **CurveMaps** name, logo or app icon (the "Marks"). The Marks
belong to Bailee Kingston.

The licence and the Marks restrict different things. The licence's noncompete
forbids providing a product that competes with CurveMaps, free or paid,
whatever it is called. This file covers the name and logo even where the
licence allows the use.

**You may:**
- say truthfully that your software is based on, or compatible with, CurveMaps;
- use the name when you discuss, review or teach about CurveMaps; and
- build CurveMaps from source for your own use, where it keeps its name.

**You may not, without written permission:**
- publish any app, build or package under the CurveMaps name, logo or icon,
  or under a confusingly similar name, in an app store or anywhere else;
- imply that your software, service or map data is official or endorsed; or
- operate a map service that presents itself as CurveMaps.

**If you distribute changed source code** (for example a fork on a code host),
say that it is a fork and isn't official. A fork may never be published as a
competing product (see `LICENSE`).

For permission, open an issue on the repository.
